Privacy & Use

This directory helps you explore faculty research and prepare for an initial conversation.

What happens when you use the directory

Searches and comparisons run in your browser. The directory does not send your searches or selections to an AI service or to the DBA program. It has no sign-in, uploads, contact form, site-added analytics or tracking cookies. Please do not enter confidential or identifiable research information.

When you save faculty, their profile IDs are stored in this browser, not in an account. To remove them, unsave the profiles or clear this site’s browser data. Comparison selections are not saved after a reload.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, even when they are not signed in. External links, including Google Scholar, are subject to the destination’s own privacy practices.

How to use the profiles

The profiles are AI-assisted summaries of publicly available sources, not statements written by the faculty themselves. Use them to find leads and prepare questions. Inclusion and search order do not indicate availability, suitability, or a commitment to supervise. Verify details with the original sources or the faculty member. This page does not grant reuse rights for profile text, images, or linked publications.

For questions about this directory or to flag a profile correction, use the Haskayne DBA program contact page.
